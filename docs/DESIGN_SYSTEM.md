## Components and behavior
Use shared header, button, eyebrow, panel, status pill, and form control conventions. All interactive controls have labels, keyboard operation, and visible focus. Amber indicates selection or active simulation, never a physical connection. Status text names simulation explicitly.

Editorial product stories use a two-column composition on desktop and collapse to one column on narrow screens. ## Responsive rules
Editorial pages use a centered max-width 1280px grid and collapse to single column. Studio stacks viewport over controls on narrow screens. Preserve reachable controls and readable type at mobile widths; avoid hover-only interactions.

Home, Studio, and technical presentation share the same CAD model and product lighting. Enclosure controls use a segmented group with pressed state and visible focus. Reduced motion removes camera travel and snaps enclosure transitions. Exterior, Interior, and Exploded describe digital-twin display states only; status labels must not imply a verified physical enclosure fit or a working hinge.
